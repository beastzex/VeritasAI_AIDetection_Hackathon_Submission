# 🎬 VERITAS AI — VIDEO DEMO SCRIPT (SYNCHRONIZED WITH SCREEN RECORDING)

Welcome to **Veritas AI** — an end-to-end, multi-signal AI admissions essay detection engine built for scientific transparency, sentence-level interpretability, and bias-aware fairness.

At the top of our studio, you can see four pre-calibrated evaluation benchmarks: from Authentic Common App personal statements to synthetic essays and non-native ESL drafts.

Unlike commercial black-box detectors that rely on brittle perplexity thresholds or hallucinating LLM queries, Veritas operates on a strict **Non-LLM Verdict Policy**. Authenticity is computed entirely through deterministic mathematics and deep learning attention.

When we paste an essay into the Workbench and click **"ANALYZE IN STUDIO"**, our backend extracts four parallel, non-LLM feature streams:

First, **Signal A — Vocabulary Signature**: Dirichlet-prior log-odds scoring that catches hallmark synthetic n-grams like *"testament to"* and *"pivotal role"*.

Second, **Signal B — Narrative Trajectory**: MiniLM dense embeddings tracking semantic velocity to expose flatline AI pacing versus authentic human narrative leaps.

Third, **Signal C — Stylometrics & Burstiness**: Tracking sentence length variance and discourse markers to actively protect human expressive pacing.

And fourth, **Signal D — Supervised Attention**: A fine-tuned DeBERTa-v3 cross-attention classifier trained on admissions corpora.

Immediately, the studio renders this interactive, DAW-inspired authenticity heatmap. The top Document Spectrum ribbon displays the overall composition, while every sentence is mapped across three distinct risk bands: **Red for High AI**, **Gray for Mixed Uncertainty**, and **Teal for Human Authenticity**.

When we click any individual sentence in the heatmap, the **Evidence Inspector** drawer opens on the right. Admissions committees can audit the raw mathematical telemetry: exact hallmark n-gram matches, narrative consistency scores, token burstiness, and DeBERTa attention confidence.

Right below, we leverage **Groq-accelerated LLaMA 3.3 70B** strictly for post-hoc translation—translating complex statistical vectors into a transparent, plain-English summary in under 400 milliseconds without ever voting on or altering verdicts.

Scrolling down to our **Architecture Terminal**, you can inspect the regularized logistic regression combiner and learned feature weights. Signal C's length burstiness parameter actively protects human writers by rewarding expressive variance.

Finally, navigating to our **Research and Proofs** audit tab reveals our held-out test evaluation across 4,496 essays: achieving **98.4% accuracy**, **0.9995 ROC-AUC**, and a **0.0133 Brier loss**. 

Crucially, on authentic non-native English learner benchmarks, Veritas achieves **96.7% specificity** with just a **3.3% false positive rate**, safeguarding international students against unfair bias.
